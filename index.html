About

Why NLP4HC?

Healthcare access in India is deeply challenged by language barriers. This workshop brings together researchers to build NLP and AI solutions that make healthcare communication more inclusive.

India's linguistic diversity — with 22 scheduled languages and hundreds of dialects — creates significant barriers in healthcare delivery. Patients often cannot communicate effectively with healthcare providers, medical records are inaccessible, and health information remains out of reach for millions.

The NLP4HC Workshop addresses this critical gap by fostering research at the intersection of Natural Language Processing (NLP), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and healthcare communication. We specifically focus on Indian languages and other low-resource multilingual settings.

This workshop is proposed under the ACL Joint Call for Workshop Proposals 2027, covering EACL, COLING, NAACL, ACL, and EMNLP 2027. Workshop assignments will be announced in October 2026.

Research Areas

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions across a broad range of topics that bridge NLP, AI, and healthcare — especially in Indian language contexts.

🏥

Clinical NLP & Information Extraction

Named entity recognition, relation extraction, and information retrieval from clinical notes and EMRs in Indian languages.

🌐

Medical Machine Translation

Translation of medical texts, discharge summaries, and patient communications across Indian languages and English.

💬

Health Dialogue Systems & Chatbots

Conversational AI for patient consultations, health queries, and symptom checking in multilingual settings.

📖

Medical Text Summarization

Automatic summarization of patient records, clinical guidelines, and health literature for accessibility.

🔍

Health Misinformation Detection

Identifying and countering health-related fake news, rumours, and misinformation in Indian social media.

📋

Medical Question Answering

Building QA systems for health information retrieval in Indian languages for patients and clinicians.

🧬

Biomedical NLP for Indian Languages

Adapting biomedical NLP tools and benchmarks to Indian language texts and code-mixed scenarios.

🔊

Speech & Spoken Health Communication

ASR, TTS, and spoken dialogue systems for healthcare in low-literacy and multilingual contexts.

🛡️

Ethics, Bias & Fairness in Health AI

Fairness, accountability, and transparency in AI systems deployed for Indian healthcare settings.

Submissions

Call for Papers

We invite original research papers, system descriptions, and dataset papers on NLP and AI for health communication in Indian and other low-resource languages.

Scope & Areas

NLP4HC invites contributions from researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Artificial Intelligence, and Healthcare — with a particular emphasis on Indian languages, multilingual settings, and underserved communities.

We welcome papers on (but not limited to) the topics listed in the Topics section, including technical papers, resource/dataset papers, and position papers.

  • Original research papers on NLP/AI for health in Indian languages
  • Dataset and resource papers for medical NLP in low-resource languages
  • System description papers for deployed healthcare NLP systems
  • Shared task system description papers
  • Position papers on ethics, fairness, and societal impact of health AI
  • Survey and review papers on multilingual health NLP
📄 Submission Guidelines 🏆 Shared Tasks

📝 Paper Categories

  • Long Papers: up to 8 pages (+ references)
  • Short Papers: up to 4 pages (+ references)
  • Shared Task Papers: up to 4 pages (+ references)
  • Extended Abstracts: 2 pages (non-archival)

📋 Submission Format

  • ACL Anthology format (LaTeX or Word)
  • Anonymous submissions (double-blind review)
  • OpenReview or ARR submission portal
  • Non-archival option available

🌏 Languages of Interest

  • All 22 scheduled Indian languages
  • Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam
  • Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia
  • Code-mixed & transliterated text

Guidelines

Submission Guidelines

Please follow ACL formatting guidelines. The submission portal link will be activated once the workshop is assigned to a conference.

📋 Formatting Requirements

  • Use the official ACL LaTeX style files or Word template
  • All submissions must be anonymized (double-blind review)
  • Long papers: up to 8 pages of content + unlimited references
  • Short papers: up to 4 pages of content + unlimited references
  • Shared task papers: up to 4 pages + unlimited references
  • Extended abstracts (non-archival): up to 2 pages
  • Figures and tables count towards the page limit
  • Font size must be 11pt (as per ACL template)

📌 Submission Policies

  • Papers must not be under review or accepted elsewhere at time of submission
  • Previously published work may be submitted as non-archival
  • ARR-reviewed papers may be committed to NLP4HC
  • Multiple submissions from the same team allowed for different tasks
  • At least one author must register and present the accepted paper
  • Findings papers from *ACL venues are welcome to present
  • Ethical considerations should be addressed in the paper
  • Contact us for accessibility accommodations

🔗 Submission Portal

  • Submissions via OpenReview or ARR (to be confirmed)
  • Portal link will be activated after workshop assignment
  • Email submission is NOT accepted
  • Create an OpenReview/ARR account in advance
📬 Questions? Contact Us

🌐 Language Policy

  • Submissions must be in English
  • Research may focus on any Indian language(s)
  • Code-mixed and transliterated data is encouraged
  • Multilingual papers covering 2+ languages are especially welcome
  • Data and examples in Indian scripts should be clearly rendered
  • Include Unicode fonts if using non-Latin scripts in PDFs
